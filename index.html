About the company

Raise Standards. Meet James.

North Carolina Building and Remodeling LLC is a veteran-owned company founded by James White, a U.S. Navy combat veteran.

Welcome to North Carolina Building and Remodeling LLC, a proud North Carolina-based, veteran-owned company founded by James White, a U.S. Navy combat veteran with a commitment to quality craftsmanship and dependable service.

With more than 50 years of combined experience in the trades and construction industry, our team brings proven expertise, attention to detail, and pride in our work to every project. From the initial conversation to the finishing touches, our goal is simple: deliver exceptional results on time, within budget, and built to last.

We believe a successful project starts with a strong relationship. That’s why communication, collaboration, and understanding your vision are at the center of everything we do. We take the time to listen, keep you informed, and make sure your needs and goals guide every decision throughout the project.

The values of family, commitment, integrity, and dedication served us throughout our military careers, and they continue to shape the way we do business today. When you work with North Carolina Building and Remodeling, you’re more than just another project. We welcome you into our family and look forward to earning your trust while bringing your vision to life.

James Owner
